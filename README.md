## Business Problem

Wanjiku runs a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and has recorded her daily sales and stock information for two weeks. She needs to understand how her business is performing, whether sales are growing, which days perform best, and which products need to be restocked. This analysis uses core Python to examine sales performance, weekly growth, weekday and weekend patterns, stock levels, and product gross margins. The findings provide practical recommendations that can help Wanjiku make better restocking and sales decisions.

## How to Run

Open the notebook in Google Colab: [Open in Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1n3U2K1HYcT0Fd-8sLVGfCbfGbBjq7MMG?usp=sharing))

## Top Three Findings

1. Total sales were **KES 46,450**, with average daily sales of **KES 3,317.86**.
2. Sales increased by **10.66%**, from **KES 22,050 in Week 1** to **KES 24,400 in Week 2**.
3. Weekend average sales were **KES 4,900**, which was **82.50% higher** than the weekday average of KES 2,685.
